## 0.1.0

The first version of Ala Modlist. Press F8, look over what is about to be shared, and get a short link to the list of mods you have installed.

- A preview before anything is sent: your mods and the game setup, with a count and a switch per category, and a title of your own.
- A privacy check runs on the exact data before it is sent: your player name, file paths and server addresses never leave your game.
- A short link in chat with open, copy and delete buttons. On the page, mods whose files are recognised get a tick and a link to their project.
- `/alamodlist` (or `/aml`) opens the preview; `/alamodlist list` and `/alamodlist delete <id>` manage your lists. A list is kept for 90 days after it was last opened.
- English, Russian and Ukrainian.
- Resource packs, shaders and server plugins come in a later version.
